**Epic**: #522 | **Spec**: `../spec.md` | **Plan**: `../plan.md` |
**Class**: test-module decomposition

## Scope

Move the embedded `binary_oracle_edge_taker` test inventory out of
`src/strategies/binary_oracle_edge_taker/mod.rs` into a test-only module tree under
`src/strategies/binary_oracle_edge_taker/tests/`.
